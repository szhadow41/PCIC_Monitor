Underwriter Submission Guidelines

  1. Complete Data Requirement: Ensure all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) are fully accomplished.
  2. Commodity Program Selection: Select at least one viable commodity program (Rice, Corn, HVC, LIV, FSH, NCI, TIP) for all field activities.
  3. Location Specificity: Clearly indicate the Region, Province, and City/Municipality for both the Agency/Institution and Venue.
  4. Travel Declaration: Specify NO TRAVEL - OFFICE DUTY or NO TRAVEL - ON LEAVE if you had no official field travel during the reporting period, were stationed at the office, on approved leave, or if Official Business (OB) was within a 50 km radius.
  5. Date Accuracy: Verify Start and End Dates to ensure there are no month or year discrepancies relative to your actual activity schedule.
  6. Reporting Deadline & Late Harmonization: Accomplishments must be submitted weekly by Tuesday, 11:59 PM. If you miss the deadline, submitting late cures the severe Unreported Travel penalty (3 pts) into a minor Late Report (1 pt). Failing to declare after 2 weeks incurs 3 points per undeclared week.
